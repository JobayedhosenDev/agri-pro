Our Agricultural Services

The primary goal of our company is ensuring your farm equipment operates at peak efficiency with guaranteed parts availability, direct engineering support, and rapid turnaround.

1. Agricultural Equipment Supply & Consultation

Selecting the wrong implement dimensions or weight can result in excessive diesel consumption, soil compaction, and premature PTO wear. Our consultation team evaluates your tractor specs, hydraulic flow, and specific soil conditions before recommending a machine.

What You Receive:

  • Horsepower and PTO RPM matching audit
  • Working width optimization for seasonal acreage throughput
  • Full compatibility guarantee on linkage categories (Cat 1, 2, 3)
Request Equipment Consultation

2. Rapid Spare Parts Supply & Dispatch

Harvest and tillage seasons don't wait. We stock over 12,000 line items at our central European distribution hub: drop-forged flail hammers, heavy tillage blades, gearboxes, PTO shafts, bearings, and hydraulic seals.

Why Choose Our Parts Service:

  • Same-day dispatch for orders placed before 14:00 CET
  • Original European OEM specifications for guaranteed fitment
  • 10-year parts guarantee on all serial numbers sold
