Our Story & Mission

Experience You Can Trust.
Solutions Built for Agriculture.

Founded with a passion for European soil stewardship, AGRI-PRO designs and distributes robust agricultural machinery built to endure generations of demanding farm life.

Engineered for European Soil & Conditions

For more than 15 years, our engineering department has observed how European farming has evolved. Farms require attachments that can withstand heavy clay, stony ground, and demanding pasture maintenance without unexpected downtime.

We avoid fragile lightweight assemblies. Each AGRI-PRO rotary tiller, flail mower, and tractor attachment is constructed with heavy-gauge European certified steel, cast-iron gearboxes, and dynamically balanced rotors tested up to 2,200 RPM.

"Our philosophy is simple: build machinery so dependable that when the tractor turns on, the farmer never has to worry about the implement behind it."

— Head of Engineering, AGRI-PRO Machinery B.V.
