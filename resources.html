FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical delivery timeframe for machinery within Europe?

Standard machines and implements currently in stock ship from our central Dutch hub within 48 hours. Transit to Germany, Belgium, France, and neighboring regions typically takes 2 to 4 business days on dedicated tail-lift transport.

Are AGRI-PRO implements compatible with all tractor brands?

Yes. All our implements adhere strictly to European ISO standards for three-point linkages (Category 1, 2, and 3) and standard 1-3/8" 6-spline 540 RPM PTO shafts. They fit John Deere, Fendt, New Holland, Massey Ferguson, Case IH, Deutz-Fahr, and compact tractor brands.

How is warranty and spare parts replacement managed?

Every machine is covered by a 24-month manufacturer structural warranty. Spare parts (blades, belts, bearings, seals) are stocked continuously and can be ordered directly by phone or via our certified dealer network with express dispatch.

Can I test or inspect the machines before purchasing?

Absolutely. Visitors are welcome at our demonstration yard in the Netherlands by appointment. Alternatively, our regional partner dealers host scheduled field demo days across Europe.
