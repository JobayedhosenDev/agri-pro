Engineering Features

The RT-1800 features a monocoque welded steel chassis designed to handle torque stress without flexing. The rear leveling board is spring-dampened to produce an ultra-fine, even seedbed surface ready for immediate sowing.

  • Forged alloy helical rotor blades for low tractor drag
  • Double-lip oil seals for dust and slurry protection
  • Lateral manual offset adjustment up to 120 mm

Primary Applications

Engineered specifically for European farming scenarios where versatile soil tillage is critical across multiple crops:

  • Vegetable Seedbed Preparation: Fine tilth creation.
  • Vineyards & Orchards: Inter-row aeration and weed control.
  • Pasture Renovation: Breaking old sod and incorporation of mulch.
